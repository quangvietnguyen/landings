Astra · Lunar Observer

Meet tonight's Moon.

Astra is an interactive 3D lunar observer for iPhone and iPad. Explore the Moon's calculated phase, travel through the lunar calendar, and view an LRO-inspired orbiter.

Phase-aware 3D Moon

See calculated illumination, Moon age, distance, and orbital geometry in a responsive lunar view.

Explore any date

Move one day at a time or reset to today to understand how the Moon changes across its cycle.

Your perspective, optionally

Foreground location can adjust the view for your hemisphere. Astra remains useful if you decline permission.

No account or ads

Astra has no sign-in, subscriptions, purchases, or advertising. See the Privacy Policy for analytics details.
