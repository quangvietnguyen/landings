Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Can I use Deepy without creating an account?
Yes! Deepy features full Offline / Guest mode. All your tasks, schedules, and streaks are stored locally on your device via SQLite. An account is only needed if you want multi-device cloud synchronization.
How does Deepy protect my privacy?
Deepy includes zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero cross-app tracking. All schedule reminders and timers are generated locally by your device operating system.
What languages are supported?
Deepy supports English, Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt), French (Français), Chinese (中文), Korean (한국어), and Japanese (日本語).
What platforms are supported?
Deepy runs on iOS (iPhone and iPad on iOS 15.0+) and Android (Android 8.0+).
