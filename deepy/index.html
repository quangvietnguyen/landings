FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Can I use Deepy without creating an account?
Yes! Deepy features a full Offline / Guest mode. Your schedules, tasks, and streaks are stored locally on your device via SQLite. You only need to sign in if you wish to sync data across multiple devices.
How does Deepy protect my privacy?
We practice strict data minimization. There are zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cross-app trackers, and all notification alerts are generated directly on your device hardware without sending schedule details to external servers.
What languages are supported?
Deepy includes native translations for English, Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt), French (Français), Chinese (中文), Korean (한국어), and Japanese (日本語).
What platforms does Deepy support?
Deepy is built for iOS (iPhone & iPad on iOS 15.0+) and Android (Android 8.0+).
